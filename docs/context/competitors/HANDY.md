## Business Model

Handy/Angi Services operates as a **fully managed marketplace** — it is not a lead marketplace (no lead-resale). The platform acts as the dispatcher and de facto employer, collecting full payment from the consumer and paying workers a predetermined rate per job.

### Revenue Structure
- Consumer books at a **fixed price** (e.g., flat rates for cleaning based on home size; hourly for handyman)
- Handy collects 100% of the consumer payment
- Handy pays the worker a predetermined rate (the margin is undisclosed — Handy calls it "marginal" but worker complaints suggest it can be severe)
- Platform does not disclose take rate publicly

### Consumer Pricing (Cleaning — illustrative)

|---|---|---|---|

*Prices vary significantly by market. Recurring pricing is the core offering.*

### The FTC Settlement Details (January 2025)
The FTC and NY Attorney General jointly filed against Handy Technologies (dba Angi Services) for:
1. **False earnings claims:** Ads stating workers could earn "up to $53/hr" when fewer than 10% achieved this. Median earnings were under $27/hr.
2. **Deceptive "daily pay" advertising:** Ads claimed daily payout availability; reality was 7-day default payout, with same-day pay only available as a paid add-on and only after completing at least one prior job.
3. **Penalty fine system:** Workers fined $50+ for issues that were consumer-initiated (consumer no-shows, consumer-cancelled bookings not processed through the system), deducted directly from worker earnings.

## Lead Resale: NO
Handy dispatches a single provider per job — it is a managed dispatch model. The consumer does not select a specific provider; Handy's algorithm assigns one. However, the worker has no control over the job price or conditions.

## Payout Speed: 7 Days Default (Expressly Deceptive — See FTC)
- **Default:** 7 days after job completion
- **Express payout:** Available for an additional fee — but only after a worker has completed at least one job on the platform first
- The FTC complaint was triggered specifically by Handy advertising "daily pay" while delivering 7-day payouts

## Recurring Bookings: YES (Core Feature)
Recurring cleaning is Handy's strongest offering. Consumers can set up recurring weekly, biweekly, monthly, or quarterly service. This is the primary consumer retention mechanism.

## Quality Verification Beyond Stars: Minimal
Background checks only. No skills verification. No structured job-completion audit. Consumer reviews on platform.

## Crew/Team Accounts: NO
Individual contractor accounts only. No team management.

## Worker Sentiment (Severely Negative)

**Dominant complaints from Handy workers (2024–2025):**
- "$50 fine charged even when the customer cancelled and didn't notify us properly"
- "I made way less than the advertised $53/hr — after fines and fees I was making $18/hr"
- "7-day payout when they advertised daily pay — that's why the FTC got involved"
- "You can't negotiate price — Handy sets it and you take it or leave it"
- "Platform takes such a large margin that the math doesn't work for building a real business"
- "No path to repeat customers — all consumer-platform relationships flow through Handy"

## HomeBase Strategic Implications

1. **Handy's managed dispatch model is directionally correct (single provider per job) but execution is a regulatory liability.** HomeBase's model differs critically: workers on HomeBase are true independent contractors with transparent, fast payouts tied to verified completion — not a managed dispatch system with punitive fines and 7-day holds.
2. **The FTC settlement is a direct talking point for provider acquisition.** HomeBase can legitimately say: "On HomeBase, you get paid same-day when the job is verified complete. No 7-day holds. No $50 fines when the customer no-shows."
3. **Recurring subscription model validation.** Handy proved that homeowners want recurring cleaning subscriptions. HomeBase's subscription-first booking flow (MVP-1) enters a market where demand for recurring bookings is already established and validated by Handy's success.
4. **Handy's narrow category scope (cleaning + handyman only) means Lawn Care is uncontested by Handy.** HomeBase's lawn care vertical has zero competitive overlap with Handy's offering.
