## What Housecall Pro Is (and Is Not)

Housecall Pro is **not a consumer marketplace**. It does not generate leads or connect homeowners with providers. It is a SaaS operational platform that home service businesses use to run their operations after they have customers.

**What it does:**
- Scheduling and dispatching
- Invoicing and payments
- Customer communication and follow-up
- GPS technician tracking
- Recurring service plans
- Job costing

**What it does not do:**
- Generate consumer demand
- Match homeowners with providers
- Provide lead generation

Housecall Pro users still need to get their own customers from Angi, Thumbtack, Google LSA, word of mouth, or (soon) HomeBase.

## Key Features

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- Drag-and-drop scheduling calendar
- Real-time technician GPS tracking on dispatch map
- Automated appointment reminders (SMS + email)
- Route optimization suggestions

### Customer Management
- Customer-facing online booking portal
- Automated follow-up sequences post-job
- Review request automation (Google, Facebook)
- Customer communication history

### Operations
- Estimate creation and digital approval
- Invoice generation with instant payment links
- Before/after photo documentation
- Job costing (actual vs. estimated)
- Chemical and materials tracking (for pest/lawn)

### Payments
- Housecall Pro Payments (Stripe-powered)
- Credit card, ACH, tap-to-pay accepted
- Settlement: 1–2 business days standard
- Instant payout available (fee-based)

### Recurring Service Plans
- Contract-based recurring service agreements
- Automated scheduling for recurring visits
- Integrated billing for recurring contracts
- Client portal for plan management

### Integrations
- QuickBooks Online (full sync)
- Google Calendar
- Zapier (workflow automation)
- Thumbtack (lead import — notable for HomeBase)

## Market Position

Housecall Pro targets the **1–8 technician** home service business — smaller than ServiceTitan's sweet spot ($3M+ revenue companies) but fully-featured for operations.

**Best for:** Companies that need strong real-time dispatching, GPS tracking, and recurring service contracts.
**Weakest at:** CRM depth and client relationship management (Jobber is stronger here).

---

## How HomeBase Relates to Housecall Pro

**At MVP:** Complementary. A Housecall Pro user still needs leads — HomeBase can be their lead source. A HomeBase provider can manage their operations in Housecall Pro and receive jobs from HomeBase.

**At Phase 4 (HomeBase OS):** Direct competitive substitute. HomeBase OS at $349/month flat (no per-tech pricing) undercuts Essentials ($149/mo + $35/user for teams of 3+) and MAX while adding marketplace demand generation that Housecall Pro cannot provide. The pitch: "One platform for demand + operations, not two subscriptions."

---

## HomeBase Strategic Implications

1. **Phase 4 pricing ($349/mo flat) is competitive against HCP Essentials + MAX for teams of 3+.** At 4 techs on HCP Essentials + $35/additional user, the monthly cost approaches $185/mo — plus the separate Angi/Thumbtack lead-gen spend. HomeBase OS at $349/mo replaces both.
2. **HCP's Thumbtack integration is a reference point.** HCP users can import Thumbtack leads directly. HomeBase should consider similar integrations to capture HCP users before Phase 4.
3. **The operational feature set HomeBase must eventually match:** Scheduling, dispatch, GPS tracking, invoicing, recurring service plans. These are table stakes for Phase 4 OS — not MVP scope.
4. **HCP's per-tech pricing creates upgrade friction.** Every additional tech costs $35/month. HomeBase OS at $349/mo flat eliminates this barrier entirely for growing teams.
