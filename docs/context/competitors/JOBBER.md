## What Jobber Is (and Is Not)

Like Housecall Pro, Jobber is **not a consumer marketplace**. It is a SaaS operational platform for home service businesses — scheduling, client management, invoicing, payment processing, and communications. Jobber does not generate consumer leads or match homeowners with providers.

**Where Jobber excels over Housecall Pro:**
- Stronger CRM and client relationship management
- Better QuickBooks Online integration
- More affordable entry point ($39/mo Core plan vs. $59/mo HCP Basic)
- Better for 5–25 tech teams

**Where Housecall Pro is stronger:**
- Real-time GPS dispatching and technician tracking
- More advanced scheduling UX
- Recurring Service Plans module is more polished

## Key Features

### Client Management (CRM — Jobber's Strongest Differentiator)
- Full client history with all jobs, invoices, and communications in one record
- Custom fields for client profiles
- Client-facing portal for booking requests and invoice payment
- Automated appointment reminders and follow-ups
- Review request automation post-job
- Referral request automation

### Scheduling
- Calendar-based scheduling with color coding by team member
- Client availability recording
- Automated appointment reminders (email + SMS)
- Recurring job scheduling

### Quoting & Invoicing
- Professional quote creation with line items
- Online quote approval by client
- Invoice generation from approved quotes
- Automated invoice follow-up sequences
- Batch invoicing

### Operations
- Job forms with required photo fields
- Mobile app for techs in the field
- Time tracking per job
- Job costing (Grow plan and above)
- Chemical tracking

### AI Features (2025 additions)
- **AI Receptionist ($99/mo):** Automated phone answering, lead capture, appointment scheduling via AI voice agent. Answers after-hours calls and captures new lead information.
- **Marketing Suite ($79/mo):** Review generation, referral requests, email campaigns to existing clients.

### Payments (Jobber Payments)
- Credit card (online): 2.9% + $0.30
- Tap-to-pay (in-person): 2.7% + $0.30
- ACH/bank transfer: 1%
- Instant payout: +1% on processing fee
- Settlement: 1–2 business days standard

### Integrations
- **QuickBooks Online:** Full two-way sync (Connect plan and above) — Jobber's strongest integration
- Google Calendar
- Stripe (alternative payment processor)
- CompanyCam (job photos)
- Gusto (payroll)
- Zapier

## Target Customer Profile

Jobber's sweet spot is home service businesses with:
- 1–25 technicians
- Service types: landscaping, cleaning, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, pest control
- Strong need for client management and recurring service contracts
- Already using or want to use QuickBooks Online

## How HomeBase Relates to Jobber

**At MVP:** Complementary. A Jobber user still needs leads — HomeBase can be their lead source. A HomeBase provider can run their operations in Jobber and receive verified, exclusive jobs from HomeBase.

**At Phase 4 (HomeBase OS):** Direct competitive substitute for the full operations layer. HomeBase OS at $349/month flat competes against:
- Jobber Grow at $199/mo (10 users) + Angi/Thumbtack lead-gen at $500–$2,000/mo = $700–$2,200/mo total
- HomeBase OS at $349/mo = marketplace demand + full operational suite — a single platform replacing two subscriptions

The key pitch: Jobber gives you software to manage jobs you still have to find. HomeBase OS gives you both the jobs (via the marketplace) and the software to manage them.

---

## HomeBase Strategic Implications

1. **The combined Jobber + lead-gen cost ($700–$2,200+/mo) vs. HomeBase OS ($349/mo flat) is a compelling price story.** At Phase 4, HomeBase's ROI calculator practically writes itself.
2. **Jobber's AI Receptionist ($99/mo add-on) is a signal.** The market is ready for AI-assisted operations. HomeBase's OS agents (Conductor, Dispatch, etc.) go much further — but Jobber validating the add-on market is a useful data point.
3. **QuickBooks integration will be expected.** Jobber's strongest integration is QuickBooks Online. HomeBase OS at Phase 4 should support QuickBooks sync — operators who switch from Jobber will expect it.
4. **Client CRM is Jobber's moat among small operators.** HomeBase OS's Operator Graph + Relations Agent must provide equivalent or superior client relationship intelligence to displace Jobber for operators who are CRM-dependent.
5. **Referral and review automation.** Jobber's Marketing Suite ($79/mo) is doing what HomeBase's Reputation Agent will do automatically — just without the cross-operator intelligence layer.
