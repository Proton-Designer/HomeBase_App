## Business Model

TaskRabbit connects consumers with independent "Taskers" for on-demand home tasks: furniture assembly, cleaning, handyman, moving help, mounting, yard work, and more. Unlike Angi/Thumbtack, TaskRabbit does NOT use a lead-resale model.

### Revenue Streams

**1. Service fee from providers (15%):** TaskRabbit charges Taskers a **15% service fee** on the total billed amount for every completed task. This is deducted automatically from the Tasker's payout.

**2. Trust & Support fee from consumers (5–15%):** Consumers pay a Trust & Support fee on top of the Tasker's quoted rate. The percentage varies by task type and market. In California and Massachusetts, this is folded into a single disclosed "Service Fee" line due to state disclosure laws.

**3. One-time Tasker registration fee (~$15):** Non-refundable background check fee paid when a Tasker joins.

**4. TaskRabbit for Business:** Corporate tier for companies needing regular services. Subscription model.

**5. Surge pricing:** Applied during peak demand periods.

### Total Platform Take
The combined provider fee (15%) + consumer fee (5–15%) can reach **30% of total job value** in some cases. Edge cases documented at 70% of the original bid when fees compound.

## Consumer Flow (Critical Difference from Angi/Thumbtack)

1. Consumer selects the **type of task** they need
2. Platform shows **matching Taskers with their profiles, reviews, and hourly rates**
3. Consumer **selects a specific Tasker** they want to work with (not a blast to multiple)
4. Tasker accepts and the job is scheduled

**This consumer-selects model is directionally similar to HomeBase** — the homeowner picks who they want, not just whoever calls fastest. However, TaskRabbit charges a 15% service fee on completed jobs and has no quality verification beyond background checks. HomeBase providers pay nothing until job completion and compete on trust score rather than hourly rate alone.

## Key Features

- Consumer selects specific Tasker (not lead auction)
- Taskers set their own hourly rates (platform provides suggested guidance)

- Background checks required for all Taskers
- Real-time location sharing during active tasks
- In-app invoice and tipping
- TaskRabbit for Business (corporate recurring services)

## IKEA Integration (Key Distribution Asset)

- **June 2023:** IKEA Canada integrated TaskRabbit assembly booking directly into the IKEA purchase checkout flow
- Now rolled out across U.S. and European IKEA markets
- Consumers buying IKEA furniture can add TaskRabbit assembly as a line item during checkout — no separate app needed
- This is a powerful distribution moat for the furniture assembly category specifically

**Implication for HomeBase:** IKEA integration locks TaskRabbit into furniture assembly. HomeBase's categories (lawn care, cleaning) are not affected by this moat.

## Lead Resale: NO
TaskRabbit does not sell the same lead to multiple providers. The consumer browses profiles and selects one specific Tasker. The selection mechanism is browse-and-pick (consumer-driven), and no provider pays to receive the inquiry.

## Payout Speed: 1–3 Business Days
Payouts arrive within 1–3 business days after task completion, processed via Stripe. No same-day payout option publicly documented.

## Recurring Bookings: YES
TaskRabbit supports recurring scheduling (weekly, biweekly, monthly). TaskRabbit for Business includes subscription-style recurring service contracts.

## Quality Verification Beyond Stars: Basic
- Background checks required for all Taskers (covered by the $15 registration fee)
- Skill-specific screening varies by category
- No structured post-job quality audit
- No composite trust scoring
- Consumer star ratings only

## Crew/Team Accounts: NO
Individual Tasker accounts only. No crew management, no multi-tech dispatch, no team hierarchy.

## Tasker Sentiment

**Dominant concerns (2024–2025):**

- "IKEA integration is great for assembly volume but rate competition is intense"

## HomeBase Strategic Implications

1. **TaskRabbit's consumer-choice model is the closest to HomeBase's philosophy among all five competitors.** The homeowner picks who they want — not a lead blast. HomeBase improves on this by surfacing trust scores built from verified completions, so the choice is informed rather than gut-feel.
2. **The gap is in quality verification.** TaskRabbit's "one Tasker" model means the consumer is making a selection based on reviews + hourly rate — no verified completion data, no composite trust score, no check-in-verified quality signal. HomeBase's check-in + completion_ledger makes that selection meaningfully more trustworthy.
3. **No crew/team support is a real gap.** TaskRabbit is built for solo 1099 workers. Small businesses (2–20 employees) have no path on TaskRabbit. HomeBase's MVP-10 (crew accounts) directly serves this segment.
4. **IKEA distribution moat is category-specific.** Furniture assembly is TaskRabbit's moat. Lawn care and cleaning — HomeBase's core categories at MVP — have no IKEA equivalent distribution lock-in.
5. **Payout speed is a differentiator.** TaskRabbit is 1–3 days. HomeBase's Stripe Instant same-day payout is faster and a direct acquisition lever for providers who value cash flow.
