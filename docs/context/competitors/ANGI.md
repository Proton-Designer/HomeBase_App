## Business Model

Angi operates three distinct monetization models:

### 1. Angi Leads (pay-per-lead) — Primary Revenue Driver
- Contractors pay **$288/year** membership fee
- On top of membership, contractors pay **$15–$100+ per lead** depending on category and geography
- Each lead is sold to **3–8 contractors simultaneously** — the first to call typically wins
- Contractors cannot see how many others received the same lead
- Leads can come from homeowners outside the contractor's stated service area
- Lead quality is inconsistent; fake/bot lead complaints are documented

### 2. Angi Ads (cost-per-click advertising)
- Contractors pay **$300+/month** for prominent placement in Angi search results
- Annual contracts with **30–35% early termination fee** and 60-day cancellation notice
- No guaranteed job volume — just visibility

### 3. Angi Services (managed dispatch — formerly Handy)
- For select categories (cleaning, handyman): Angi acts as provider of record
- Consumer books at a fixed price; Angi dispatches a single provider
- Angi collects payment, takes margin, pays provider
- Default payout: 7 days post-completion
- This model was acquired via the Handy acquisition (October 2018, ~$165.5M stock)

## Lead Resale: YES (Core Business Model)
Every lead is sent to 3–8 contractors simultaneously. Contractors pay per lead regardless of whether they win the job. Estimated lead cost per realistic shot at winning: $200–$250.

## Payout Speed: N/A (Leads Model) / 7 Days (Angi Services)
In the standard Leads/Ads model, providers collect payment directly from the homeowner. In Angi Services, default payout is 7 days post-completion.

## Recurring Bookings: Partial
Available in Angi Services (cleaning, handyman) via repeat scheduling. Not available in standard Leads model — each inquiry is a fresh transaction.

## Quality Verification Beyond Stars: Minimal
Optional background check. No skills verification. No job-completion confirmation. No structured quality audit. Consumer reviews only.

## Crew/Team Accounts: NO
Individual contractor accounts only. No multi-tech dispatch or team management features.

## Provider Sentiment

**Dominant complaints from contractors (2024–2025):**
- "Leads are shared with 3–8 other contractors" — standard complaint
- "Fake leads / leads from outside my service area" — leads are charged even for fake inquiries
- "Terrible ROI — paying $800/month and winning 0–2 jobs" — common in competitive markets
- "Annual contract is a trap" — 30–35% early termination penalty, 60-day cancellation notice
- "Call within 2 minutes or you lose the job" — whoever calls fastest wins, not the best contractor

## Recent Strategic Changes

- **April 1, 2025:** IAC completed spin-off of Angi as an independent public company (NASDAQ: ANGI). IAC shareholders received Angi shares directly.
- **January 2025:** Joey Levin transitioned from IAC CEO to Executive Chairman of Angi; Jeff Kip remains Angi CEO; new CPO Kris Boon appointed.
- **2025 strategy:** Pivoting away from pure lead-resale toward Angi Services (managed booking) and advertising models. Attempting to reduce dependence on the declining Leads business.
- **2026 guidance:** Company targets return to revenue growth in 2026.

## HomeBase Strategic Implications

1. **Angi's model cannot be reformed without destroying their P&L.** The lead-resale model accounts for the majority of Angi's revenue. Pivoting to completion-based would eliminate their core margin. They are structurally trapped.
2. **Provider attrition is accelerating.** Active pro count down 17% YoY — providers are leaving. This is a direct pipeline for HomeBase provider acquisition.
3. **The FTC action validates HomeBase's positioning.** A platform that earns only on verified completion is not just a product choice — it is a compliance-safe posture while Angi faces regulatory headwinds. HomeBase never charges providers to receive or respond to a request.
4. **Angi's brand is damaged.** C+/F BBB ratings, "Pattern of Complaints," FTC fine — consumer trust is eroding. A new platform with transparent, verified quality scoring has clear air to fill.
